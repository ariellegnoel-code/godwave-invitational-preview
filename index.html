THE GATHERING

The GodWave Invitational is a highly curated gathering of influential leaders who lead with faith, character, and purpose inside of culture and industry.

This one-day experience brings together individuals shaping business, culture, sports, and community through high conviction and service.

A private convergence of faith, influence, and culture.

“A rare convening of culture’s coolest leaders committed to making the world better.”

With cash prizes and a focus on meaningful impact, this event sets a new standard for industry-driven tournaments.

THE TOURNAMENT

Hosted at Mulholland Hills Country Club, the tournament will feature a full day of golf and friendly competition.

  1. 01

    Team Scramble (Foursome)

    A competitive team format emphasizing camaraderie and strategy.

  2. 02

    Longest Drive powered by The Rainmaker

    Truly see the longest drives with the Darts Only activation, equipped with Rainmaker technology.

  3. 03

    Darts Only Challenge powered by Smartpin

    Determines the Closest to the Pin (CTP) winner and awards players with major cash prizes.

  4. 04

    Live On-Course Content and Performance

    Influencers, artists, and livestreamers actively engaging with guests on course.

  5. 05

    Tournament Prizes

    Awards for winners across all competition categories.
