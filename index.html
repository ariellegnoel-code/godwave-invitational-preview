THE GATHERING

"When two or more gather."

The GodWave Invitational was built on a simple conviction: the people shaping industries, culture, and communities deserve a room, and a course, worthy of the conversations that happen there. Every field is curated. Every pairing is intentional. What happens over eighteen holes is the beginning, not the point.

THE EXPERIENCE

Three things every Invitational holds

Championship Play

Storied courses and a field small enough that every group actually knows one another by the turn.

Sacred Fellowship

Devotion and real conversation woven through the day, off the leaderboard, off the record.

Kingdom Purpose

A share of every Invitational's proceeds is given directly to the organizations on this page.

FAMILY & COMMUNITY

An elite, culture-shifting gathering that feels like family.

The GodWave Invitational is intentionally family-centered. Guests are encouraged to bring spouses and family members to enjoy the event experience together: a gathering that celebrates leadership, faith, and community in a space where families belong.
